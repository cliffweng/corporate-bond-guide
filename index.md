# Corporate Bond Guide

A practical, desk-level study guide for Penn builders, Wharton quantitative finance students, and candidates preparing for credit research, leveraged finance, and fixed-income interviews. It covers the core analytical toolkit: price/yield mechanics, duration and convexity, credit spread decomposition, ratings, capital structure and seniority, covenants, default and recovery, and relative value — with clean math and zero fluff.

## How to use this guide

- Each topic in the navigation is a **standalone page readable in ~10 minutes**.
- Every page follows an identical, interview-tested shape: **why it matters**, **core concepts** with explicit formulas, an intuitive **mental model** (ASCII diagram), **interview questions** with detailed answer keys, and a curated **watch list** of verified videos where a good one exists.
- Topics are sequenced logically from price/yield fundamentals through credit mechanics to relative value and interview synthesis. You can work through them linearly or jump directly to specific interview hotspots.
- Interview badges highlight prep priority: **🎯 Frequent** (core credit/FI technicals) and **Occasional** (specialized or situational topics).
- Nothing here requires an external service, database, or sign-up — just clean, scannable Markdown.
